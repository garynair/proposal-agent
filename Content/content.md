## Slide 2 — Executive Summary

- Current state: ABC Electronics receives ~300 customer queries per day across WhatsApp, email, and its website, handled entirely manually by 8 full-time support agents, with 4–6 hour response times and no after-hours coverage.
- Proposed solution: An AI Customer Support Agent spanning FAQ automation, order tracking, returns/refunds, human escalation, and analytics, deployed across the website, WhatsApp, and email channels.
- Expected value: 80% of responses automated, response time under 5 minutes, 30% reduction in support workload, improved customer satisfaction.
- Implementation duration: 6 weeks, delivered across 5 phases.

(Source: 01, 02, 05)

## Slide 5 — Proposed AI Solution

1. **AI FAQ Agent** — Automatically answers repetitive product, order, and warranty questions.
2. **Order Tracking Assistant** — Gives customers instant order status without agent involvement.
3. **Returns & Refund Assistant** — Automates returns and refund queries and guidance.
4. **Human Escalation Workflow** — Routes complex cases to human agents when automation can't resolve them.
5. **Website Chatbot** — Front-line AI support on the website channel.
6. **WhatsApp AI Assistant** — Extends automated support to the WhatsApp channel.
7. **Daily Support Analytics Dashboard** — Gives management visibility into volume, automation rate, and performance.

(Source: 02. Note: the Slide 5 guide names 5 modules; all 7 components from doc 02 are included here — the guide's list is illustrative, not exhaustive.)

## Slide 6 — Solution Architecture

Flow: Customer → Website / WhatsApp / Email → AI Agent (FAQ, Order Tracking, Returns) → Knowledge Base → Business Systems (order/inventory data) → Human Agent (if escalation needed). The Analytics Dashboard draws data across this flow for reporting.

Integration points implied by the solution: Knowledge Base (Phase 1), website chat widget (Phase 2), WhatsApp Business integration (Phase 3), order-tracking/business systems data source, Analytics Dashboard (Phase 4).

(Source: 02, 05)
